Quality and inspection

Checked in Egypt, before it ever reaches you.

We work only with packers we have vetted, and we check each lot against the agreed specification before it leaves Egypt. If it doesn't match, it doesn't ship.

  1. 01

    Vetted packers

    We visit and assess packers in Egypt before we buy from them: facilities, hygiene, documentation and track record.

  2. 02

    Agreed specification

    Variety, grade, size, moisture, packaging and labelling are written down and agreed with you up front.

  3. 03

    Pre-shipment inspection

    Each lot is inspected and photographed before loading, with results shared with you.

  4. 04

    Cold chain and paperwork

    Reefer containers for frozen goods, and the export and import documents prepared for Canadian entry.
